About us

The first link in the build chain

Aggregated Aggregates is a specialised operating division of Besbpo Group. We supply graded sub-base, crushed stone, sand, crusher run and specialised aggregates to civil, commercial, industrial and residential projects, and we have grown the range to include cement, ready-mix concrete, reinforcing and structural steel, plant hire and site services.

The name carries two meanings: aggregated, one supplier consolidating a fragmented category, and aggregates, the product itself. Every layer of every build starts with what we deliver.

We work as a broker and network: an approved supplier network across South Africa supplies the material, and vetted partners supply the machines and trucks. You deal with one platform, one order and one point of contact.

FAQ

Common questions

Where do you deliver?

We launch in KwaZulu-Natal and Gauteng and are expanding to other provinces. Delivery is priced by load size and distance band, and sites beyond our standard bands are quoted.

Can the public buy, or only contractors?

Both. Retail customers can order online. Contractors can apply for a trade account, and volume or civil bulk buyers can request a quote.

How do I pay?

Card, instant EFT and bank EFT, trade-account purchase orders, and selected buy-now-pay-later options. The checkout recommends methods that suit the order.

How does plant hire work?

Hire is wet hire: the machine comes with an operator, fuel and PPE. You book and pay on the platform, the nearest vetted partner accepts the job, and the partner is paid after you sign it off. Rates are quoted until partner rates are published.

How do I know how much material I need?

Use the project estimator for volume, weight and truck loads. It is a guide only, so confirm quantities with your engineer.

I run a quarry, plant-hire or haulage business. Can I work with you?

Yes. Apply through the partners page. We ask for company and tax details, insurance, operator certificates, an equipment list and written rates.
